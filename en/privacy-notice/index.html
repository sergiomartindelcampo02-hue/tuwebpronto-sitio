Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-11-10

This English version is provided for convenience. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

1. Data controller

Grupo Ortopédico de Mexicali, located at Av. Francisco I. Madero 1616, Col. Nueva, 21100 Mexicali, B.C., Mexico, is responsible for the use and protection of your personal data under Mexico's Federal Law on the Protection of Personal Data Held by Private Parties and other applicable regulations.

2. Personal data we collect

  • Identification and contact: name, phone, WhatsApp number and email.
  • Website form data: name, phone, email (optional), area or reason for your visit (optional) and any message you write.
  • At your appointment: the data needed for your care and to issue tax receipts when you request them.

Sensitive personal data: to care for you we may collect information about your health (for example, the reason for your visit, symptoms, imaging and medical history). This data is protected with enhanced security measures and processed only with your express consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form or in writing at your appointment.

3. How we obtain your data

Directly from you: through this website's form, WhatsApp, phone or in person.

4. Purposes

Necessary purposes:

  • Handling your request, scheduling, confirming and following up on appointments.
  • Assessment, diagnosis, treatment and medical follow-up, and keeping your clinical record as required by Mexican regulation NOM-004-SSA3-2012.
  • Billing and issuing tax receipts.
  • Contacting you about changes to your appointment or for follow-up after your care.

Additional purposes: we do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it. If we ever want to send you information or promotions, we will ask for separate consent.

5. Who we share your data with

We may share your data without your consent where the law allows it, for example with laboratories, imaging centers or other healthcare professionals when needed for your diagnosis or treatment, with your insurer for procedures you request, and with competent authorities when required by law.

We also use providers that help us run this website. Some of them process data outside Mexico:

  • Netlify, Inc. (United States): website hosting and receipt of the contact form, acting as a processor on our behalf only.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms): if you choose to message us on WhatsApp, the message is also subject to that service's terms.
  • Google: the Google Maps map loads only if you click “Show map”.

6. ARCO rights and withdrawal of consent

You have the right to Access your data, Rectify it, Cancel it or Object to its use (ARCO rights), and to withdraw your consent or limit the use of your data. To do so, contact us by phone and WhatsApp +52 686 355 9793 or in writing at our address. Your request must include your name and a way to reply to you, a copy of your ID (or your legal representative's), a clear description of the data and the right you wish to exercise, and any document that helps locate the data.

We will reply within 20 business days and, if granted, your request will take effect within the following 15 business days. Clinical records must be kept for the period set by health regulations, so some data cannot be cancelled during that time.

7. Cookies

This website uses one necessary cookie to remember your cookie choice. Third-party tools are only activated with your permission. See our Cookie Policy and change your choice at any time in .

8. Changes to this notice

We will publish any change on this page with the date of the last update.
