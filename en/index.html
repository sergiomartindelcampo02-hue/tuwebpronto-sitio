FAQ

What people ask us before getting started

I already have Instagram. Why do I need a website?

People searching Google for your service find websites, not profiles. And if the algorithm changes or your account gets shut down, your website is still yours.

Do you guarantee I'll rank first on Google?

No, and be wary of anyone who promises that. What we do is build your site the right way for Google: titles, descriptions, map and business info. That's the foundation for getting found.

Will my website look the same as another business's?

No. Every site is designed with your colors, logo, photos and copy, and we keep a record so we never reuse the same design for two businesses in the same industry in your town.

What if I don't have photos or copy?

We'll tell you exactly what you need. If you'd rather, we can write the copy for you as an add-on, and if you're in Mexicali we can do a photo and video shoot, quoted separately.

Is the domain registered in my name?

Yes. We register your .com in your name from day one, in an account you own. We cover the first year, and after that you renew it yourself. You'll never have to ask us to transfer it.

Do I have to pay monthly?

No. The package is a one-time payment of $797 USD. If you'd like us to maintain the site for you, we'll talk it through and send you a quote.

Need it faster?

We offer express delivery in 72 hours as an add-on. Ask us on WhatsApp if there's a spot open this week.
