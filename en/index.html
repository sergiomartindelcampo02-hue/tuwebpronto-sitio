Services

What do we treat at Grupo Ortopédico?

Assessment, diagnosis and treatment of injuries and pain in bones, joints, muscles and ligaments.

Knee

We assess knee pain, swelling and the feeling that the knee gives way, whether it comes from a recent injury or from wear.

Common reasons for a visit

  • Pain on stairs or when standing up
  • Meniscus or ligament injury
  • Joint wear (osteoarthritis)
  • Swelling after a twist
More about knee

Hip

We treat hip and groin pain, stiffness and difficulty walking, sitting or putting on your shoes.

Common reasons for a visit

  • Groin pain or pain on the side of the hip
  • Stiffness when walking or getting up
  • Joint wear
  • Tendon or bursa inflammation
More about hip

Shoulder

We assess shoulder pain, loss of strength and difficulty raising your arm or sleeping on that side.

Common reasons for a visit

  • Rotator cuff injury
  • Tendinitis and bursitis
  • Stiff or frozen shoulder
  • Dislocation or instability
More about shoulder

Spine

We assess neck and lower back pain, stiffness and pain that spreads into the arms or legs.

Common reasons for a visit

  • Lower back pain
  • Neck pain
  • Herniated disc
  • Pain radiating down the leg (sciatica)
More about spine

Sports injuries

We treat sprains, strains, tendinitis and ligament injuries in active people, with a plan for a gradual return to activity.

Common reasons for a visit

  • Ankle or knee sprain
  • Muscle strains
  • Overuse tendinitis
  • Ligament injuries
More about sports injuries

Fractures

We assess and treat fractures and suspected fractures, and follow up while the bone heals and mobility returns.

Common reasons for a visit

  • Wrist, ankle and other fractures
  • Follow-up with a cast or splint
  • Fractures from falls
  • Review of fractures that are not healing
More about fractures

Guidance

When should you see an orthopedic doctor?

A consultation is worth it if you have any of these symptoms:

  • Pain that has lasted more than a few days and does not improve with relative rest.
  • Swelling after a blow, a twist or a misstep.
  • Difficulty moving a joint or bearing weight.
  • A feeling that the knee, ankle or shoulder slips out or gives way.
  • Tingling, numbness or pain running down an arm or a leg.
  • A previously treated injury that keeps bothering you.

When not to wait for an appointment

If there is obvious deformity, exposed bone, you cannot bear weight after a fall, the limb turns cold or pale, or you lose strength or bladder control, go to an emergency room or call 911.

Frequently asked questions

What does an orthopedic and trauma specialist do?

It is the medical specialist in bones, joints, muscles, ligaments and tendons. They diagnose and treat injuries such as fractures, sprains and tears, and conditions such as joint wear or spine pain, with conservative treatment or surgery depending on each case.

What should I bring to my appointment?

An ID, any imaging you already have —X-rays, MRIs and their reports—, a list of the medications you take and, if applicable, notes from other doctors. Wear comfortable clothing that allows the area to be examined.

Do all bone and joint injuries require surgery?

No. Many are treated without surgery, with relative rest, medication, immobilization or rehabilitation. Surgery is considered when the diagnosis calls for it; during the consultation we explain the options for your case.
